**Deprecated, and removed after one release.** Use `AppListRow` instead: same
row, fixed slots, and a chevron on every row that navigates. `is="danger"` is
`tone="danger"` there, and `is="header"` is `AppList heading`.

Content that is none of leading, title, meta or trailing was never a list row.
It belongs in `AppOptionRow`, `AppPreferenceRow`, or a widget of its own.
