# AI-generated promo assets

These fictional training images were generated with the built-in image generation model for the One More Rep promotional reel. They intentionally contain no text, logos, or identifiable real people. The app UI, logo, typography, gradients, and watermark are composited by `render_reel.swift`.

## `workout-squat.png`

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical 9:16 hero frame for a premium fitness app promo reel
Primary request: Create a lifelike, cinematic editorial photograph of a fictional athletic woman performing a controlled barbell back squat in a modern independent strength gym.
Scene/backdrop: charcoal steel racks, real rubber flooring, subtle depth and gym atmosphere; no visible brands.
Subject: strong but realistic build, excellent squat form, full body visible, forest-green training top, dark neutral leggings.
Style/medium: photorealistic high-end sports campaign photography, authentic skin texture and effort, not glossy CGI.
Composition/framing: vertical portrait, low three-quarter side angle, subject in lower-middle of frame, generous darker negative space in upper third for promo copy, 35mm lens feel.
Lighting/mood: dramatic warm gold rim light with soft natural fill, focused, determined, aspirational.
Color palette: charcoal black, deep forest green, restrained warm gold highlights.
Constraints: fictional adult person; anatomically correct hands, limbs, barbell and plates; safe believable technique; no text, no logos, no watermark.
Avoid: neon colours, bodybuilding stage aesthetic, sexualized pose, exaggerated muscles, extra limbs, warped equipment, crowded gym.
```
